What is Project Design?

  • A practice that not only envisions, designs and executes projects, but also takes the methods of execution and management themselves as its object, and envisions and designs them.
  • It looks through two lenses, “what we do” (Doing) and “what is happening” (Being). The two look at one whole from different sides.
  • It mainly applies to profit and non-profit enterprises. To research, life and other endeavors, it extends by structural likeness.
  • A large way of organizing it is the reader “What I Want to Call Love-Driven Development β”.

What I want to call Love

  • Love is not limited to romantic love. For now it is placed in two aspects: the aspect of a force by which people and things feel drawn, and the aspect of a property of the field that people are in.
  • The analogy is the relation between gravity and spacetime. Neither aspect is chosen over the other; both are set side by side as aspects.
  • Many definitions have been given to the word Love. pjdhiro sees them as possibly all being right.

4

Answer for now

We may be inside something like a field we want to call Love, and part of it. The body senses the reassurance of being connected, and gaps from it. Feeling those gaps as a lack, people start to move. The driving source of projects may be this lack, the gap between what I want to call Love and oneself.

The grounds are one person's plain experience (pjdhiro's theory from plain experience), not statistics from studying and counting many people. Research is cited not to prove the experience but as material for conveying it.

Four explorations of the invisible driving source

The driving source cannot be seen directly. Just as gravity has been written through how other things move, it is written through how the things it moves move.

What Is Creation

What structure and process does something follow when it comes into being? Theories from 30 fields, set side by side and written in five stages (Field → Wave → Edge → Vortex → Bundle).

What Is Consciousness

What do people cut the world with? An exploration through two signals: whether we can survive, and whether we can stay connected (trust).

Kesson-Driven Thinking

What do people sense where symbols read zero? An exploration of a stance of mind driven by a sense of lack or unease.

Is Love Like Gravity?

The center of a gravity well does not move, holds nothing and cannot see itself, yet everything turns around it. A reader that asks whether love is like that.

The five stages of creation

Field

場

A stable state, like the sea before waves rise.

Wave

波

Differences arise and face each other.

Edge

縁

Unlike things meet and collide.

Vortex

渦

Two stay distinguishable, yet hold together as if one.

Bundle

束

A gathering that remains as a new form, and becomes the next field.

The five stages do not necessarily proceed in order; they run at once and enter into one another. The definitions are provisional, and are filled in by reading theories from 30 fields.

What it is good for

The view of Love-Driven Development is pursued in the belief that it contributes to creation, including design and growth, to asking questions, and to value judgment and decision-making. All of these are seen as especially needed in the age of AI.
