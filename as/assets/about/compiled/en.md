# What Is Consciousness

What do we cut the world with?

The reading on Love-Driven Development looks for what moves people and projects (the driving source). This page is one of those explorations, and it looks at the receiving side.

People may cut the world by anticipating two values: whether they can survive, and whether they can stay connected (trust). The two are easier to see when written apart, yet they are not separate; they are mistaken for each other and woven into each other. Consciousness, too, may be one of the names given to a shape cut out this way.
